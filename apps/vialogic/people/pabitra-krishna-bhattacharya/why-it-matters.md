# Why it matters

## WHY IT MATTERS

**FACT:** Bhattacharya’s documented work asks a practical chemical question: how does changing the structure around a metal change what that metal complex can do? His papers measured the stability of mixed-metal-ligand systems and tested iron Schiff-base complexes as catalysts for turning alkenes into epoxides. [C007–C011]

**INFERENCE:** That is still a live design principle in chemistry. A catalyst is not only “a metal”; its ligands, geometry, and number of cooperating metal centers can alter stability and reactivity. [C016]
