## Abstract
Professor and Head of Chemistry at M.S. University of Baroda for 34 years.
Fulbright-Hays Fellow (Ohio State, 1976–77). Published on metal complex stability,
bioinorganic coordination chemistry, and iron-catalysed alkene epoxidation.
Co-authored *Metal Ions in Biochemistry* (CRC Press, 2nd ed., 2020).
