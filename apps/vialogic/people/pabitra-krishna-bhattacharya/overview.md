# Pabitra Krishna Bhattacharya

**FACT:** Pabitra Krishna Bhattacharya was a chemist at M.S. University of Baroda, where a publisher biography identifies him as a retired Professor and Head of Chemistry; the Chemical Research Society of India independently lists his full name at that department. [C001, C002] His documented research subjects include coordination chemistry, bioinorganic chemistry, and homogeneous catalysis. [C005] His papers examined mixed-ligand metal complexes, ternary-complex stability, and Schiff-base complexes used in olefin epoxidation. [C006–C011] He also coauthored the second edition of *Metal Ions in Biochemistry*. [C012]

**FACT:** Birth date and death date are **not established in available sources**.
