# Timeline

- **1976–1977 — FACT · REPORTED:** A publisher author biography reports that Bhattacharya held a Fulbright-Hays senior fellowship at Ohio State University. [C004]
- **1979 — FACT · REPORTED:** With B. T. Thaker, he published a study of mixed-ligand Schiff-base complexes of copper(II) and nickel(II). [C006]
- **1985 — FACT · REPORTED:** With V. K. Patel, he published formation-constant measurements for ternary copper systems involving substituted catecholates. [C007]
- **1985 — FACT · REPORTED:** With P. J. Patel and V. K. Patel, he compared ternary nickel and copper complex stability in a bioinorganic research question. [C008]
- **1989 — FACT · REPORTED:** He coauthored an iron(III) Schiff-base study of alkene epoxidation using iodosylbenzene. [C009, C010]
- **1990 — FACT · REPORTED:** He published a paper on homogeneous olefin-epoxidation catalysis by mono- and binuclear Schiff-base complexes. [C011]
- **2001 — FACT · REPORTED:** The publisher biography says he retired as Professor and Head after 34 years at M.S. University of Baroda. [C002]
- **2020 — FACT · REPORTED:** CRC Press published the second edition of *Metal Ions in Biochemistry*, coauthored with Prakash B. Samnani. [C012]
